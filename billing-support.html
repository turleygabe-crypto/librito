Billing support

Your reading life should feel cozy, including managing your subscription. Here's how Book Lover billing works.

support@librito.casa

Your Book Lover plan

Book Lover is offered at $2.99 USD per month or $24.99 USD per year. Applicable taxes, if any, are shown at checkout. Check the checkout summary before subscribing.

A subscription renews automatically at its selected interval unless you cancel. The plan page shows your renewal date or, if canceled, when your remaining access ends. Stripe securely processes payments; don't send payment details by email.

Open your plan and billing settings

Cancel a subscription

  1. Sign in to Librito and open your Book Lover plan from the Account menu.
  2. Select Cancel subscription and confirm in the Librito dialog.
  3. Check the confirmation and access end date. Cancellation stops future renewals; Book Lover remains available through the paid period.

You can also cancel through Manage billing. After cancellation, your books and shelves stay saved. Lite Reader limits apply to adding new items once paid access ends.

If you change your mind before access ends, Renew subscription restores automatic renewal only after you confirm. Once a subscription has ended, use Resubscribe to Book Lover to start a new one.

Payment methods, receipts, and failed payments

Use Manage billing to open Stripe's secure portal, update payment methods, or view and download invoices. You don't need to share your card information with support.

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe may retry it. Access may remain available during retries and ends if the subscription becomes unpaid or canceled. Check your plan status and update your payment method promptly.

Refunds and billing errors

We do not routinely refund unused portions of a billing period or renewal charges. Cancellation does not automatically issue a refund or remove an outstanding balance.

If you believe a charge is incorrect, duplicated, or unauthorized, or you have exceptional circumstances, contact support@librito.casa so we can review it. Include your account email, charge date, amount, and an invoice reference if available, but no full card details.

This approach does not limit any cancellation, withdrawal, refund, or other consumer rights required by applicable law.

Deleting your account is different

Normal cancellation keeps your account and remaining paid access. Delete account permanently removes your account and library, cancels your subscription immediately, and forfeits remaining paid access. It does not automatically refund a payment. Save anything you want to keep first.

Deletion stops future subscription renewals but does not reverse payments already made or erase legally required financial records. An existing payment may already be processing. Contact support if deletion fails or you have a question about a pending charge.
