This is our contact for customer support, technical help, billing, privacy questions, and accessibility feedback. Messages are reviewed by a person; we don't promise a particular response time.

For account help, email from the address you use for Librito when possible. Include a brief description of the problem, your device/browser, and any relevant error message. Please redact private information in screenshots.

Never email passwords, sign-in codes, full card numbers, security codes, or API keys. We won't ask for them.

Privacy and your account

To ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, email a privacy request. We may need to verify account ownership before responding or making changes.

You can permanently delete your account from the Account menu in Librito. Choose Delete account, read the warning, and type DELETE to confirm. This stops your subscription immediately and removes your account and library; it is not the same as canceling renewal.

Save information you want to keep before deleting. Remaining paid access is lost and no automatic refund is issued, without limiting any rights required by law. Financial records, support correspondence, security logs, and backups may remain where required or necessary; contact us with questions about your circumstances.

If you cannot sign in or deletion cannot finish, contact support. If billing has already stopped but deletion failed, retry deletion or ask us for help.
